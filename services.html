Questions

What is in the written build plan?

A hardware recommendation or a check of the shortlist you already have, a network and VLAN plan, the storage and backup approach, the order I would build it in, and the pitfalls I would watch for in your specific setup. It is written so you can follow it on your own.

Do you work on business environments?

No. The design review is for personal labs, home offices and prosumer setups. For a production business environment you need an IT partner who can support it day to day, and Mylemans Online does not provide managed services.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the size of the setup, so it is quoted on request. You get one fixed price before anything is booked, with no hourly billing and no surprises afterwards.

Can you build it for me?

No. You get the plan and the reasoning behind it, and you build it yourself. The blog and the free paths on Mylemans Labs cover most of the individual steps.
